# human-like-writing

Write the piece one specific person would actually write. The default AI register (smooth,
hyped, evenly structured, decorated with emoji and bold labels) is itself the giveaway.
Replace it with a single human voice making one real claim.

## Decide three things before the first sentence

- **Voice.** One real person talking to one real reader. Not "a thought leader." Write the
  way that person talks: contractions, plain words, first person.
- **Claim.** The one thing this piece asserts. Say it to yourself in a sentence first. If you
  can't, there is nothing to write yet.
- **Shape.** Order the points the way the idea needs them, not intro → three bullets →
  takeaway → call to action. Most posts need no recap and no "thoughts?" closer.

## Write it like this

- Open on the actual point or a concrete detail. No hook question, no "Imagine…", no "Here's
  the thing."
- Prose paragraphs. Use a list only for genuinely list-like content (a few benchmark numbers),
  and at most once.
- Vary sentence length. Let one run long and the next land short. Even rhythm is the loudest
  tell a reader catches.
- Back the claim with one concrete fact or number. Cut the second adjective.
- End on a real last point, not a summary and not a "what do you think?" prompt.

## Cut these on sight (each one marks text as AI)

- **Em dash (—).** Use a comma, a period, or parentheses. Do not substitute a colon; readers
  flag that now too.
- **Emoji** anywhere: as bullets, in headers, decorating lines.
- **Bolded label bullets** (`**Speed:**` …, `**Why it matters:**` …). Write the plain sentence
  without the label.
- **Hashtag blocks**, and the hype words: game-changer, unlock, leverage, dive into, deep dive,
  supercharge, must-watch, massive, revolutionary, seamless, elevate. Say plainly what it does.
- **Hypophora**, asking your own question to answer it: "The problem? …" "The results? …".
  State it directly.
- **"Not just X, it's Y"** and its tail form **"Y, not just X"** ("scores went up, not just the
  cost"). Say what happened straight: "scores went up and the cost dropped." Drop reflexive
  rule-of-three triads too.
- **Meta and packaging.** Output only the piece. No "here are three options," no preamble, no
  "let me know if you'd like changes," no horizontal-rule dividers.

## Do not over-correct

Dodging the tells with choppy three-word fragments, forced all-lowercase, or fake typos is its
own tell. The target is a real voice that means what it says, not a costume of not-AI.
